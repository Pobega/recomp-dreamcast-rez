Game Information
Title: Rez
URL: https://github.com/Pobega/rez-portmaster

Instructions:
Convert your Japanese (HDR-0178) or European (MK-5119250) Dreamcast Rez V1.003 disc to .chd and copy it to ports/rez/gamedata/. First launch takes about 15 seconds to verify the disc and build the executable.

| Button | Action |
|--|--|
|DPAD| Move Reticle / Navigate Menus |
|Left Analog| Move Reticle |
|A| Lock-on / Shoot / Confirm |
|B| Overdrive / Cancel |
|X| Lock-on / Shoot |
|Start| Pause / In-Game Menu |
|Select| Controller Bindings Menu |
|Select + Y| Toggle FPS Counter |
|Select + Start| Exit Game |
